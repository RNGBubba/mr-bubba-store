## Pricing Tiers

### 🥉 Standard Template — $25
**Best for:** Simple tracking, personal use, small datasets

Included:
- Single-sheet workbook
- 10-15 pre-configured columns
- Basic formulas (SUM, AVERAGE, COUNT, IF)
- Input cell highlighting (yellow)
- Calculation cell highlighting (green)
- Auto-totals row
- Professional header/footer formatting
- Print-ready layout
- PDF delivery format

Templates available: Budget Tracker, Invoice Tracker, Inventory List, Expense Log

**Delivery:** Within 2 hours

---

### 🥈 Premium Template — $35
**Best for:** Multi-department tracking, growing businesses, reporting needs

Includes everything in Standard, PLUS:
- Multi-sheet workbook (2-3 sheets)
- 15-25 pre-configured columns
- Advanced formulas (VLOOKUP, INDEX/MATCH, SUMIF, COUNTIF)
- Summary/dashboard sheet with cross-sheet references
- Conditional formatting (overdue alerts, stock warnings)
- Date calculations (days outstanding, aging)
- Data validation dropdowns
- Chart/graph placeholders
- Named ranges for easy navigation

Templates available: Project Manager, Sales Pipeline, Payroll Calculator, Inventory Manager

**Delivery:** Within 2 hours

---

### 🥇 Enterprise Template — $50
**Best for:** Complex operations, multiple stakeholders, full automation

Includes everything in Premium, PLUS:
- Multi-sheet workbook (3-5 sheets)
- 25-40 pre-configured columns
- Complex nested formulas (IFS, SUMIFS, ARRAY)
- VBA macros for automation (buttons, auto-refresh)
- Custom branding with company colors/logo placement
- Automated email triggers (via VBA)
- Multi-currency support
- Scenario modeling (what-if analysis)
- Data connection templates (CSV import/export)
- Comprehensive documentation sheet

Templates available: Full Financial Dashboard, CRM Pipeline, Supply Chain Tracker, Multi-Entity Payroll

**Delivery:** Within 2 hours
