Local-first accessibility QA

Check common accessibility signals without uploading page code.

A small browser bookmarklet for developers. It inspects the page you are already viewing and reports heuristic findings locally. It does not establish WCAG conformance, ADA compliance, or legal compliance. The landing page is hosted on GitHub Pages; normal page requests may be logged by the host.

10 focused checks

Images, headings, labels, links, language, duplicate IDs, tabindex, button names, viewport, and main landmark.

No backend

The bookmarklet does not fetch, upload, store, or transmit the inspected page.

Human review required

Heuristics miss keyboard, screen-reader, cognitive, and context-specific issues.
