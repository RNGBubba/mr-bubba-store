## 🎯 What We Need From You

To compile your lead list, please provide:

1. **Industry/Niche** — e.g., "SaaS", "Restaurants", "Dental Practices"
2. **Location** — e.g., "New York, NY", "Texas", "United Kingdom"
3. **Company Size** — Small (1-50), Medium (51-200), Large (200+), or Any
4. **Keywords** — Optional: specific technologies, specialties, or criteria
5. **Quantity** — How many leads you need (determines tier)
