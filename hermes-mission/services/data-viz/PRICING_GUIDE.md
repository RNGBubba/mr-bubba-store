## 💡 Tips for Best Results

1. **Clean data** = better charts. Remove blank rows/columns before sending.
2. **Include headers** in the first row for automatic column naming.
3. **Note any preferences** in your email (colors, chart types, focus areas).
4. **Large datasets** (>10,000 rows) may benefit from specifying aggregation preferences.
5. **Multiple sheets** in Excel? Mention which sheet(s) to visualize.
